BODYRON Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

BODYRON is a workout tracking application designed to help users record workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, RIR, body weight, and progress over time.

Information We Collect

In the current version of BODYRON, workout and progress information entered by the user is stored locally on the user's device. BODYRON does not require an account or registration.

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information through the current version of the app.

How We Use Information

Information entered into BODYRON is used to provide workout tracking, history, progress calculations, and related app functionality.

Data Sharing

The current version of BODYRON does not sell or share users' workout data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Advertising

BODYRON does not currently display third-party advertising.

In-App Purchases

BODYRON may offer optional paid digital features through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. BODYRON does not receive or store users' full payment card details.

Data Security

Because workout information in the current version is stored locally on the device, users should protect access to their device. If future versions introduce cloud storage, accounts, analytics, or other data collection, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

BODYRON is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact
